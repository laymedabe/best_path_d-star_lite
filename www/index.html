      ⛑️ Survival & First Aid Guide

      🌊 Flood Protocol

      • Move immediately to higher ground. Do not wait for instructions if waters are rising rapidly.
      • Do not walk through moving water. Six inches of moving water can make you fall.
      • Do not drive into flooded areas. If floodwaters rise around your car, abandon the car and move to higher ground.
      • Disconnect electrical appliances. Do not touch electrical equipment if you are wet or standing in water.

      🏚️ Earthquake Protocol

      • DROP, COVER, and HOLD ON! Drop to your hands and knees. Cover your head and neck with your arms. Hold on to any sturdy shelter.
      • Stay away from glass, windows, outside doors and walls, and anything that could fall.
      • If outdoors, stay outdoors. Move away from buildings, streetlights, and utility wires.

      🩹 Basic First Aid

      • Bleeding: Apply direct pressure to the wound with a clean cloth. Keep pressure applied until bleeding stops.
      • Burns: Cool the burn under cold running water for at least 10 minutes. Cover loosely with sterile dressing.
      • Fractures: Immobilize the injured area. Do not attempt to realign the bone.

      How to Use This App

      1. Select a Route: Use the dropdown on the left panel to choose your starting location (Barangay).
      2. View Path: The best evacuation path to the town plaza will automatically calculate and draw in blue.
      3. Block Roads: If a road is impassable (flooded, landslide), tap the red Block button next to the route name. The map will instantly recalculate a new alternative path (in yellow).
      4. Manage Blockages: Open the left panel and scroll to "Blocked Roads" to unblock any roads that are clear again.
      5. View History: Tap the 📜 History button on the top right to view all your previously generated evacuation routes and distances.

      Note: This app runs completely offline. You can zoom in and twist the map using two fingers.
